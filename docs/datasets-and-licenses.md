## Corpus construction and integrity notes

- The controlled corpora are byte-matched at 33,554,432 UTF-8 bytes per corpus (`CORPUS_BYTES`) and passed byte-match verification (`BYTE_MATCH_OK`). See [`../data/audit/audit_summary.json`](../data/audit/audit_summary.json) and [`../publication/claim_evidence.csv`](../publication/claim_evidence.csv).
- The general-English exact-deduplication pass removed 134 exact duplicates (`DEDUP_GENERAL_EXACT`).
- Cross-corpus near-duplicate filtering removed 9 evaluation items near training content (`CROSS_CORPUS_REMOVED`).
- The frozen seed is 20260709 (`SEED`) for sampling, splitting, and bootstrap procedures.

## Redistribution policy

- **Raw corpora are not redistributed.** The repository publishes scripts, configs, manifests, hashes/provenance, and aggregate results. Raw corpora and source clones are git-ignored; see [`../licenses/RESTRICTIONS.md`](../licenses/RESTRICTIONS.md).
- **Azure-only tokenizer artifacts are distributable with attribution.** They were trained solely on the CC-BY-4.0 Azure documentation anchor and must carry the attribution text in [`../licenses/ATTRIBUTION.md`](../licenses/ATTRIBUTION.md). Microsoft product names and trademarks are not licensed, and no endorsement is implied.
- **General and Mixed tokenizer artifacts are local-use-only.** They involve WikiText-derived content and are not approved for redistribution in this release.
- **WikiText licensing remains conservative.** The Hugging Face metadata and card prose disagree between CC-BY-SA/GFDL and CC-BY-SA version wording. The licensing review pinned the dataset README revision `b08601e04326c79dfdd32d625aee71d232d685c3`, while the frozen acquisition lock records `hf:main` at acquisition time. Raw WikiText and WikiText-derived tokenizer artifacts remain vetoed/local-use-only pending legal review.

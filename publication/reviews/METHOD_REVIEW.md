2. Add a small dedup sensitivity note/table: threshold `0.8`, first-6,000-character shingling cap, number of eval removals, and why over-/under-dedup is unlikely to change the principal conclusion.
3. Keep the matched general-technical training control prominent as the highest-priority follow-up; it is the key missing baseline for interpreting whether the effect is Azure-specific.
4. Optionally report final-document truncation counts from byte matching (Azure=1, General=1, Mixed halves=1+1) to make exact byte matching transparent.

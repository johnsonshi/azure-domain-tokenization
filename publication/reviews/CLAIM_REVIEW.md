## BLOCKERS

1. **Citation mismatch in related work.** Reword `paper/sections/02_related_work.tex:24-29` so BioBERT and CodeBERT are not cited as examples of work that "trains tokenizers" unless the sentence distinguishes domain model pretraining from tokenizer/vocabulary training. Suggested direction: "Prior work trains domain models and, in some cases, domain vocabularies..." then cite SciBERT/LegalBERT/KL3M separately from BioBERT/CodeBERT.
2. **Claim registry overstates numeric coverage.** Either register the unregistered public numbers in `publication/claims.yaml`/`claim_evidence.csv` or weaken the paper statements from "Every number" to "Every reported result number" and clarify that method constants/source file counts/runtime-environment descriptors are documented but not registry-verified.

## Non-blocking wording nits

- Replace or quantify `paper/sections/07_analysis.tex:7-11` wording: "substantially less" / "much larger fraction".
- Consider softening cost-adjacent framing in `paper/sections/01_introduction.tex:5-8` and `paper/sections/09_ethics_and_licenses.tex:18-20` if the target venue is extremely sensitive to billing implications, although current disclaimers are adequate.
